About

Digital transformation consulting and training, grounded in real delivery experience.

NextEmTech is led by Deeb Haddad, a certified trainer and training consultant based in Amman, Jordan, with expertise in project management, AI, CPMAI, and AI strategy.

Work is delivered as a freelancer, contracted through training centers and Authorized Training Partners (ATPs), in both Arabic and English professional contexts — covering consulting engagements on digital transformation and AI adoption, and certification training for project managers, PMO professionals, and technology leaders.

AI provides the analysis. The person owns the decision.

That principle shapes every engagement: AI is treated as a tool for surfacing better decisions, not a replacement for the judgment and accountability of the people running the program.
