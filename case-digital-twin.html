Cognitive Project Operations & Delivery Intelligence

From Project Telemetry to Agentic Decision Intelligence

ProjectCore Telemetry AI

Enterprise AI Agentic architecture, including AI Agents implementation.

This application fuses two disciplines usually reported separately — traditional project management (schedule, cost, risk, milestones) and DORA delivery metrics (deploy frequency, lead time, change fail rate, recovery time, rework rate) — into a single Command Center. An AI Agent correlates signals across both and surfaces what a program manager should investigate next.

Every AI call is a single, stateless request — no tools, no autonomous loop, no memory of prior runs. Each response is a labeled, timestamped advisory a human reviews before acting. The AI analyzes; the person decides.

AI provides the analysis. The person owns the decision.
