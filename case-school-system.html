School System

Cloud-Based Learning & School Management System

Project Manager

The Problem

Multiple schools operated with fragmented academic and administrative systems and limited digital learning infrastructure, needing a unified, secure, cloud-based platform.

The Leadership

Developed the governance framework and full project management plan. Engaged executive, academic, IT, vendor, teacher, student, and parent stakeholders. Applied a hybrid methodology — predictive for infrastructure and security, Agile for configuration and feature delivery.

The Result

Full rollout completed across all five schools, with improved process standardization, data visibility, and digital learning adopted program-wide.

The Success Story

What started as five schools running on disconnected, paper-heavy processes became one unified digital campus network. Teachers stopped chasing spreadsheets. Parents gained real-time visibility. The transformation that looked ambitious on a charter document became the everyday normal in the classroom.
